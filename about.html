About TechCelic

Four specialists.
One interlaced studio.

We started TechCelic on a simple premise: AI and SaaS is only as useful as the product, brand and automation woven around it. So we kept every discipline in-house , no hand-offs between agencies, no lost context.

Our story

Built by engineers who got tired of the hand-off.

TechCelic began as three freelancers passing the same client back and forth , one building the automation, one the storefront, one the brand , and losing a week of context at every hand-off. We merged into one studio so a project keeps its shape from first workshop to launch day.

Today that studio runs four specialists across strategy, growth, AI engineering and automation, all working from the same brief, the same timeline, and the same definition of done.
